About Sembcorp
Sembcorp is a leading energy and urban solutions provider headquartered in Singapore. Led by its purpose to drive energy transition, Sembcorp delivers sustainable energy solutions and urban developments by leveraging its sector expertise and global track record.
Role Overview
We are looking for a hands-on Senior Data Engineer with 5+ years of data engineering experience and strong practical experience with Databricks.
You will be part of our Data & AI Innovation team, building reliable and scalable data solutions while exploring better ways to automate recurring engineering and BAU activities using Databricks, AI and agentic capabilities.
We value people who are open and honest, share ideas and knowledge, challenge constructively, collaborate with the team and experiment meaningfully with new approaches.
Key Roles and Responsibilities
Design, develop and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines using Databricks, PySpark, Python, SQL and Delta Lake.
Build and support data solutions following Lakehouse and medallion architecture principles.
Integrate data from databases, APIs, files and streaming, or IoT sources.
Implement data quality, reconciliation, incremental processing, schema management, and recovery mechanisms.
Monitor and troubleshoot production pipelines, data freshness, performance, and reliability issues.
Identify repetitive BAU activities and develop automation or agentic solutions to reduce manual effort.
Explore Databricks and AI capabilities such as Genie, MCP, LLMs and agents where they can meaningfully improve engineering and operational workflows.
Participate in technical discussions, propose practical solutions, share ideas, and experiment with new approaches as a team.
Apply good software engineering and DevOps practices, including testing, logging, version control and CI/CD.
Qualifications & Experience
5+ years of hands-on data engineering experience.
2-4 years of hands-on Databricks experience preferred. Candidates with less Databricks experience but strong practical knowledge of Spark, PySpark, SQL and modern Lakehouse architectures are also encouraged to apply.
Strong hands-on skills in Python, PySpark and SQL.
Experience with Apache Spark, Delta Lake and Databricks Jobs or Workflows.
Solid understanding of Lakehouse and medallion architecture.
Experience building and supporting production-grade ETL/ELT pipelines.
Experience with cloud data platforms such as Azure, AWS or GCP.
Good understanding of data quality, monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance optimization.
Experience with, or a good understanding of, DevOps practices, including Git, CI/CD, testing and deployment processes.
Strong problem-solving skills with an interest in improving and automating existing processes.
Experience with Unity Catalog, Auto Loader, Lakeflow, Structured Streaming or Databricks Asset Bundles.
Experience with cloud-native data and integration services.
Exposure to streaming technologies such as Kafka, Event Hubs or MQTT.
Exposure to LLMs, AI agents, MCP, Databricks Genie, RAG or Model Serving.
Experience automating data-platform monitoring, troubleshooting, or operational workflows.
Relevant Databricks certification.
Hands-on and enjoys solving real engineering problems.
Works well as part of a team and openly shares ideas, knowledge and lessons learned.
Can discuss different approaches and propose practical solutions.
Looks at repetitive work and asks whether it can be simplified or automated.
Is curious about new technologies and willing to experiment meaningfully while keeping production reliability in mind.
Values honesty, collaboration, continuous learning and constructive discussion.
Our Culture at Sembcorp
At Sembcorp, our culture is shaped by a strong set of shared behaviours that guide the way we work and uphold our commitment to driving the energy transition.
We foster an institution-first mindset, where the success of Sembcorp takes precedence over individual interests. Collaboration is at the heart of what we do, as we work seamlessly across markets, businesses, and functions to achieve our goals together. Accountability is a core principle, ensuring that we take ownership of our commitments and deliver on them with integrity and excellence. These values define who we are and create a workplace where our people can thrive while making a meaningful impact on driving energy transition.
Join us in making a real impact!
