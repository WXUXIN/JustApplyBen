About the Role
We are looking for an experienced
Data Migration Consultant
to support a large-scale digital transformation project within the public sector.
In this role, you will be responsible for executing end-to-end data migration activities, ensuring the accurate, secure, and seamless migration of data across enterprise systems. You will work closely with business stakeholders, functional consultants, and technical teams to understand data requirements, perform data mapping and transformation, and ensure data quality throughout the migration lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities
Participate in end-to-end data migration activities, from data assessment and preparation through to migration execution and post-migration validation.
Analyse existing data structures and source systems to understand data requirements and migration dependencies.
Perform data extraction, cleansing, transformation, mapping, and loading activities.
Support data migration involving Oracle platforms and/or SAP SuccessFactors.
Develop and maintain data mapping documents, migration specifications, and data transformation rules.
Conduct data quality checks, validation, reconciliation, and issue resolution to ensure accuracy and completeness of migrated data.
Work closely with business users, functional consultants, and technical teams to gather requirements and address data-related challenges.
Support migration testing activities, including mock migrations, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and cutover preparation.
Identify data discrepancies, investigate root causes, and recommend appropriate solutions.
Prepare migration documentation, status reports, and technical deliverables.
Provide post-migration support to ensure successful data transition and system stabilisation.
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related discipline.
3+ years of experience in data migration, data conversion, or enterprise system implementation projects.
Must have hands-on experience in at least one of the following:
Oracle Data Migration / Oracle database migration.
SAP SuccessFactors data migration and implementation.
Good understanding of data migration processes, including data extraction, transformation, cleansing, mapping, loading, and validation.
Experience with data quality management, data reconciliation, and migration testing.
Ability to analyse complex data structures and troubleshoot data-related issues.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work with both technical and business teams.
Good documentation and problem-solving skills.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience working on large-scale enterprise transformation or system implementation projects.
Previous experience supporting government or public sector technology projects.
Familiarity with ERP, HRMS, or enterprise application migration.
Exposure to ETL tools, SQL, or data integration technologies.
Oracle or SAP-related certifications would be an advantage.
